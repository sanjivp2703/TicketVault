📋 Manual Ticket Operations Guide

🎯 Your Daily Workflow

🔍 Step 1: Monitor Transactions

Look for transactions with status:

  • "waiting_for_payment" → Payment pending
  • "both_received_processing" → Ready for ticket transfer!
  • "ticket_forwarded_funds_held" → Waiting for buyer confirmation

📧 Step 2: Check Email

Monitor: safetransactiontix@gmail.com

  • Look for ticket transfer emails
  • Match ticket details to transaction
  • Verify event, section, row, seat

⚡ Step 3: Process Ticket Transfer

  1. Verify ticket matches listing (event, section, row, seat)
  2. Forward ticket to buyer via their preferred method
  3. Click "Declare Ticket Sent" button below
  4. Click "Pay Seller" button to release funds
  5. Send confirmation emails to both parties

🚨 If Something Goes Wrong

  • Ticket doesn't match: Use "Refund Buyer" button
  • Seller doesn't send ticket: Use "Refund Buyer" button
  • Buyer complains: Check complaint section below
  • Technical issues: Contact support
