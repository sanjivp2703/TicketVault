Platform overview

Safe Transaction is a secure escrow platform specifically designed for ticket resellers. We guarantee full payment to sellers and the promised ticket to buyers, or their assets back.

  • Guaranteed payment

    You're guaranteed to receive payment once tickets are delivered. Our escrow system protects your earnings from fraud.

  • Buyer verification

    We verify all buyers and their payment methods before allowing them to purchase, ensuring legitimate transactions.

  • Seller protection

    Our dispute resolution system protects sellers from fraudulent buyers and ensures fair resolution of any issues once proof is provided, as outlined below.

Full protection guarantee

If a user provides proof that they were scammed, resulting in the loss of their ticket or the equivalent ticket price, and they completed the transaction through our platform as outlined on our website, we will reimburse them for the ticket price (or the amount lost).

Complete process guide

Our 6-step process ensures secure transactions and guaranteed payments for sellers. Here's exactly how it works.

  1. 1
    Seller

    Step 1: Create your listing

    Sellers start by submitting a listing with event information.

    • Select the event
    • Set your ticket price
    • Enter the buyer's email (Michigan Athletics email for UMich tickets)
  2. 2
    Seller

    Step 2: Submit your tickets

    Transfer your ticket to us to verify it.

    • Go to the appropriate ticket transfer page (e.g. Michigan Athletics for UMich).
    • Enter our information and transfer the ticket:
      First name
      Safe
      Last name
      Transaction
      Email
      safetransactiontix@gmail
    • Our system automatically verifies ticket authenticity.

    If anything goes wrong in the process, we'll send the ticket back to you. If that's not possible, we will supply the ticket price.

  3. 3
    Buyer Within 1 hour

    Step 3: Buyer makes payment

    Once the ticket is verified, we'll email the buyer a payment link.

    • The buyer has to pay within 1 hour, so that the seller is not left waiting without ownership of their ticket.

    If full payment is not submitted within an hour, the ticket is sent back to the seller and we no longer accept payment.

  4. 4
    Safe Transaction Within an hour of payment

    Step 4: Automatic delivery

    Once payment is confirmed, tickets are automatically delivered to the buyer.

    • Delivery within an hour of payment confirmation
    • Buyer receives the original ticket email with all attachments
    • Transaction security maintained throughout the process
    • Buyer can access mobile tickets or PDFs within an hour
  5. 5
    Seller and buyer

    Step 5: Seller gets paid and buyer gets their ticket

    Once the buyer submits payment, the seller will be paid plus a 10% bonus on Safe Transaction.

    • The seller receives the price of the ticket plus 10% in their balance, shown on the homepage.
    • The seller can choose to withdraw funds from their Safe Transaction account, and the money is sent through the available options.
    • The ticket is transferred to the buyer's account (e.g. Michigan Athletics for UMich).

    At any point after this until 24 hours after the start of the event, the buyer or seller can submit a complaint if a scam has occurred.

  6. 6
    Seller or buyer If needed

    Step 6: Dispute resolution (if needed)

    Our platform is made to prevent any possible scam from occurring. In the off chance that this happens, follow this procedure. The goal is for the impacted user to be fully compensated.

    • File a complaint from the homepage or send an email to safetransactiontix@gmail.com with the subject line: Filing a Dispute
    • We will email both parties and request specific proof from each based on the case.
    • We will be impartial in our decision.
    • In the unlikely scenario that funds or a ticket are proven to have been taken through a scam and can't be recovered, we will provide repayment in the form of the price of the ticket and the fraudulent user will be banned from our platform.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Safe Transaction cost for sellers?
Safe Transaction is completely free for sellers, and you actually earn an extra 10% bonus on successful sales. There are no listing fees, no hidden charges, and no deductions from your ticket price.
What if my tickets are rejected during verification?
If tickets don't pass verification, you'll be notified of the failure with the specific reason. We will send the ticket back to the original seller. The listing will be cancelled, but you can submit another listing with the correct ticket.
How long does the verification process take?
Most tickets will be verified within an hour. If you don't receive a verification result (it would be shown on the homepage), email us and we'll return the ticket to you.
What happens if the buyer doesn't pay?
If payment isn't received by the deadline (1 hour after ticket verification), the transaction automatically cancels. We will send the ticket back to you and you can create a new listing immediately. There's no penalty for unpaid transactions.
Can I cancel a transaction after creating it?
Once you send the ticket to us, unfortunately you can't cancel it, as the buyer is able to purchase the ticket at that point. However, if you sent the wrong ticket it will be automatically returned. If you would like to cancel the transaction, inform the buyer so that they won't pay before the deadline. The transaction will then be cancelled and the ticket sent back to you.
