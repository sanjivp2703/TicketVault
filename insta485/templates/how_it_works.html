Platform Overview

Safe Transaction is a secure escrow platform specifically designed for University of Michigan football ticket sellers. We guarantee payment to sellers and eliminate the risk of scams by verifying buyers and holding payments securely.

Guaranteed Payment

You're guaranteed to receive payment once tickets are delivered. Our escrow system protects your earnings from fraud.

Buyer Verification

We verify all buyers and their payment methods before allowing them to purchase, ensuring legitimate transactions.

Seller Protection

Our dispute resolution system protects sellers from fraudulent buyers and ensures fair resolution of any issues.

If a user provides proof that they were scammed—resulting in the loss of their ticket or the equivalent ticket price—and they completed the transaction through our platform as outlined on our website, we will reimburse them for the ticket price (or the amount lost), up to one claim per day.

Complete Process Guide

Our 6-step process ensures secure transactions and guaranteed payments for sellers. Here's exactly how it works:

1
Create Your Listing

Sellers start by creating a detailed listing with event information, ticket details, and buyer contact.

  • Fill out complete event details (date, venue, section, row, seat)
  • Set your ticket price (what you'll receive plus 10% bonus)
  • Enter buyer's email address for automatic notifications
  • Set deadlines for ticket submission and payment
2
Submit Your Tickets

You'll receive a secure email address to forward your original ticket email for verification.

  • Receive unique secure email (e.g., tx-000001@safetransaction.com)
  • Forward your original ticket email from the venue/platform
  • Our system automatically verifies ticket authenticity
  • Never send tickets directly to the buyer's email
3
Buyer Makes Payment

Buyer receives payment notification and submits payment through our secure portal.

  • Buyer gets automatic email with payment instructions
  • Multiple payment options: credit card, bank transfer, digital wallet
  • Funds held securely in escrow, not released to seller yet
  • Payment deadline automatically enforced
4
Automatic Delivery

Once payment is confirmed, tickets are automatically delivered to the buyer.

  • Instant delivery within minutes of payment confirmation
  • Buyer receives the original ticket email with all attachments
  • Transaction security maintained throughout the process
  • Buyer can immediately access mobile tickets or PDFs
5
You Get Paid

After a 24-hour verification window, you receive payment plus a 10% success bonus.

  • 24-hour window for buyer to report any issues
  • No complaints = automatic payment release
  • Receive 10% bonus on top of your ticket price
  • Funds transferred directly to your account
6
Dispute Resolution (If Needed)

In rare cases of disputes, our team provides fair mediation to resolve issues.

  • Collect evidence and documentation from both parties
  • Impartial review by trained dispute resolution team
  • Fair decision based on evidence and platform policies
  • Money goes to the rightful party with full explanation

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Safe Transaction cost for sellers?
Safe Transaction is completely free for sellers - you actually earn an extra 10% bonus on successful sales. There are no listing fees, no hidden charges, and no deductions from your ticket price.
What if my tickets are rejected during verification?
If tickets don't pass verification, you'll be notified immediately with the specific reason. You can resubmit corrected tickets or cancel the transaction. No penalties are applied for invalid tickets.
How long does the verification process take?
Most tickets are verified within 2-4 hours during business hours. Complex cases may take up to 24 hours. You'll receive updates throughout the process.
What happens if the buyer doesn't pay?
If payment isn't received by the deadline, the transaction automatically cancels. Your tickets remain yours and you can create a new listing immediately. There's no penalty for unpaid transactions.
Can I cancel a transaction after creating it?
You can cancel anytime before the buyer makes payment. Once payment is received, the transaction can only be cancelled through our dispute resolution process.
What types of tickets do you support?
We support all University of Michigan football tickets including mobile tickets, PDF tickets, and physical tickets. We verify tickets from official sources like the Athletic Department and major platforms.
