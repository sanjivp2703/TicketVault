## Overview

Safe Transaction is committed to facilitating secure ticket transactions between buyers and sellers. This refund policy outlines when refunds are available, how they are processed, and what protections are in place for both parties.

---

## When Refunds Are Available

### ✅ **Full Refund Scenarios (Buyer Protected)**

Buyers receive a **full refund** in the following circumstances:

1. **Seller Never Sends Ticket**
   - If the seller fails to transfer the ticket within the specified deadline
   - Refund issued immediately upon confirmation
   - Processing time: 3-5 business days

2. **Invalid or Fake Ticket**
   - Ticket does not work at the event entrance
   - Ticket is fraudulent or counterfeit
   - Requires proof (photo/screenshot at event)
   - May include additional $20 compensation for inconvenience

3. **Wrong Event/Game**
   - Seller sent ticket for completely different event than listed
   - Must be significantly different (not just different section)
   - Requires proof of mismatch

4. **Payment Deadline Expired**
   - Buyer did not complete payment within 1-hour window
   - Transaction automatically cancelled
   - No charges applied

5. **Technical Transfer Failure**
   - Ticket cannot be transferred due to Michigan Athletics system issues
   - Not the seller's fault, but buyer is protected
   - Verified with Michigan Athletics

6. **Event Cancellation**
   - Official event cancellation or postponement
   - Before the scheduled event date
   - Requires official confirmation

---

### 💰 **Partial Refund Scenarios**

Partial refunds may be issued in these situations:

1. **Minor Listing Inaccuracy**
   - Seat location slightly different than described (same section)
   - Partial refund of $10-30 depending on discrepancy

2. **Late Ticket Delivery**
   - Ticket sent but arrived very close to event time
   - Buyer experienced stress/inconvenience
   - Partial refund of $10-20 as goodwill

3. **Communication Issues**
   - Both parties share responsibility for confusion
   - Mediated settlement
   - Amount determined case-by-case

---

### ❌ **No Refund Scenarios**

Refunds are **NOT available** in these circumstances:

1. **Buyer Changed Mind**
   - Buyer no longer wants to attend
   - Buyer found cheaper tickets elsewhere
   - **Solution:** Buyer can resell through Safe Transaction

2. **Buyer Provided Wrong Email**
   - Ticket sent to email address buyer specified
   - Buyer's Michigan Athletics email was incorrect
   - Buyer responsible for retrieving from Michigan Athletics

3. **Event Already Occurred**
   - Ticket was valid and transferred
   - Event has already taken place
   - Complaint filed after event date

4. **Buyer Attended Event**
   - Ticket worked and buyer entered event
   - Cannot claim ticket didn't work after attendance
   - Verified through Michigan Athletics records

5. **False Complaint**
   - Investigation proves buyer's complaint is unfounded
   - Buyer received valid ticket but claims otherwise
   - May result in account suspension

6. **Price Disagreement**
   - Buyer agreed to price when purchasing
   - Price changes after agreement not valid reason
   - All sales are final once payment is complete

---

## Seller Protections

### ✅ **Seller Gets Paid When:**

1. **Ticket Successfully Transferred**
   - Buyer receives ticket
   - Buyer completes payment
   - No complaints filed within 24 hours after event

2. **False Complaint Denied**
   - Buyer files invalid complaint
   - Investigation proves seller fulfilled obligations
   - Payment released immediately

3. **Buyer Payment Issues**
   - Buyer's payment method fails after ticket sent
   - Seller protected if acted in good faith
   - Safe Transaction mediates resolution

### ❌ **Seller Doesn't Get Paid When:**

1. **Ticket Never Sent**
   - Seller failed to transfer ticket
   - Buyer refunded, seller receives nothing

2. **Fraudulent Ticket**
   - Seller knowingly sold invalid/fake ticket
   - Buyer refunded, seller permanently banned
   - May face legal action

3. **Wrong Ticket Sent**
   - Seller sent significantly different ticket than listed
   - Buyer refunded, seller warned/suspended

## Refund Process & Timeline

### **Step 1: Complaint Filed**
- Buyer contacts support@safetransaction.app
- Complaint logged in system
- Initial response within 2 hours

### **Step 2: Investigation (24-48 Hours)**
- Review transaction history
- Check Michigan Athletics records
- Contact both parties for information
- Gather evidence (screenshots, emails, etc.)

### **Step 3: Decision**
- Support team makes determination based on evidence
- Both parties notified via email
- Decision explained with reasoning

### **Step 4: Refund Processing**
- **Immediate:** Refund initiated in Safe Transaction system
- **3-5 Business Days:** Funds appear in buyer's account
- **Method:** Refund to original payment method (credit card, etc.)

### **Step 5: Follow-Up**
- Confirmation email sent when refund completes
- Transaction marked as resolved
- Account notes updated

## Special Circumstances

### **Payment Deadline Expiration (1 Hour)**
- Buyers have exactly **1 hour** to complete payment after ticket verification
- After 1 hour:
  - Payment link automatically expires
  - Transaction cancelled automatically
  - Ticket returned to seller
  - No charges applied to buyer
  - Both parties notified

### **Event Date Protections**
- Complaints must be filed within **24 hours after the event**
- After 24 hours, transactions are considered complete
- Seller receives payment automatically
- Exceptions only for fraud or system errors

### **Dispute Resolution**
- Safe Transaction makes final determination on all disputes
- Decisions based on:
  - System records and timestamps
  - Michigan Athletics transfer logs
  - Stripe payment records
  - Communication history
  - Photos/screenshots submitted
  - Pattern of behavior (repeat offenders)

### **Partial Refund = Determined by Issue**
- Minor issue: $10-20

2. Detailed description of issue
3. Supporting evidence (screenshots, emails, photos)
4. Timeline of events
5. What resolution you're seeking

### **Contact:**
- **Email:** support@safetransaction.app
- **Subject Line:** "Refund Request - Transaction #[ID]"
- **Response Time:** Within 2 hours (acknowledgment)
- **Resolution Time:** 24-48 hours (decision)

### **What NOT to Do:**
- ❌ Contact seller directly
- ❌ Dispute charge with credit card before contacting us
- ❌ File false claims
- ❌ Wait more than 24 hours after event

3. ✅ **Verify Email:** Double-check buyer's Michigan Athletics email
4. ✅ **Confirm Transfer:** Ensure ticket left your account
5. ✅ **Respond Quickly:** Reply to support inquiries within 24 hours

## Fraud Prevention

### **Buyer Fraud:**
- Making false claims about ticket validity
- Attending event then claiming ticket didn't work
- Providing wrong email then blaming seller
- **Consequence:** Account suspension, no future refunds

### **Seller Fraud:**
- Selling same ticket multiple times
- Sending invalid/fake tickets
- Never sending tickets
- **Consequence:** Permanent ban, no payment, possible legal action

---

## Exceptions & Edge Cases

### **System Errors:**
- If Safe Transaction system causes issue, buyer protected
- Full refund + compensation
- Seller also compensated

### **Michigan Athletics Issues:**
- If MA system fails, not seller's fault
- Buyer still refunded
- Seller keeps payment if acted in good faith

### **Force Majeure:**
- Natural disasters, pandemics, etc.
- Handled case-by-case
- Following Michigan Athletics' official policies
