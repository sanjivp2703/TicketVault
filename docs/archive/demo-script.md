# 🎬 DEMO SCRIPT - TicketVault Platform

## 🎯 **2-MINUTE COMPLETE DEMO**

### **Setup (30 seconds)**
1. **Start Flask:** `flask run --port 8000`
2. **Open Browser:** `http://localhost:8000/dev/skip-login/seller`
3. **You're logged in as seller automatically**

---

### **Demo Flow (90 seconds)**

#### **Step 1: Create Listing (30 seconds)**
**Say:** *"Let me show you how easy it is to list tickets securely"*

1. **Fill out form:**
   - Event Name: `Taylor Swift Concert`
   - Event Location: `Madison Square Garden`
   - Date/Time: `December 15, 2025 8:00 PM`
   - Price: `$250`
   - Buyer Email: `buyer@example.com`

2. **Click:** `Create Listing & Send to Buyer`

**Result:** ✅ Popup appears with secure email address

---

#### **Step 2: Simulate Ticket Sending (20 seconds)**
**Say:** *"Normally the seller would email their ticket to this secure address, but let me show you our instant verification system"*

1. **In the popup, click:** `TEST: Mark as Verified`
2. **Confirm the action**

**Result:** ✅ Instant verification, buyer notification sent

---

#### **Step 3: Show Buyer Experience (30 seconds)**
**Say:** *"The buyer immediately gets a secure payment link"*

1. **Check email** (or simulate): Buyer gets payment notification
2. **Click payment link** from email
3. **Show Stripe checkout page**

**Result:** ✅ Professional payment interface

---

#### **Step 4: Complete Transaction (10 seconds)**
**Say:** *"After payment, the ticket is automatically forwarded to the buyer"*

1. **Complete payment** (or show the interface)
2. **Show success page**

**Result:** ✅ Complete automated transaction
